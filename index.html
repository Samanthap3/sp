PILOT STUDY

Human–AI Interactions in Craftwork Environments

A design-based research study using a 2-hour e-textile patch workshop to observe how 10-15 participants of varying skill levels co-create with AI tools — and what happens to creativity, decision-making, and confidence in the process.

Role: Research Assistant under Prof. Kyungwon Koh — helped shape research methodology, questions, and target subjects, and co-organized the workshop with a 7-person research team.

Early insights: AI tends to function as a supportive collaborator rather than a replacement, participants often turn to peers over AI in maker settings, and visual demonstration outpaces chat-based guidance for learning.
